THURSDAY, 19 NOVEMBER 2026
ASIA-PACIFIC · DIPLOMACY

APEC: Xi greets Trump and Putin before their first trilateral summit

Chinese President Xi Jinping, US President Donald Trump and Russian President Vladimir Putin exchanged greetings at the APEC summit in Shenzhen ahead of a group photo with world leaders. The three are due to hold their first-ever trilateral summit on Thursday.

UKRAINE · WAR

Crimean Bridge attacked a third time this week, Russian official says

Attacked by Ukraine for the third time this week, a Russian official said. Traffic was temporarily suspended after another strike in the early hours of Thursday; the bridge had already been hit twice earlier in the week, part of it collapsing into the water.

MIDDLE EAST · WAR

Trump says Iran seeks new weapons; Houthis fire at Riyadh

Trump claimed Iran is seeking chemical and biological weapons. Houthi rebels fired a missile towards Riyadh; the Saudi-led coalition says it responded and is assessing damage from falling debris.

USA · TECH / BUSINESS

Anthropic valued at up to $1.5tn ahead of its market debut

Shares priced at $83 each start trading on the Nasdaq on Thursday. The first US firm focused exclusively on AI to go public; the offering was more than ten times oversubscribed.

THE HAGUE / MYANMAR · COURTS

ICJ to rule on Rohingya genocide case against Myanmar

The court rules Thursday on Gambia's case accusing Myanmar of genocide after a 2017 crackdown that displaced over 730,000 Rohingya. It could be the first ruling that a state committed genocide.

USA · HEALTH

Unvaccinated Pennsylvanian dies amid historic measles resurgence

An unvaccinated Pennsylvania resident has died after contracting measles, the eighth measles-related fatality in the state this year -- a day after the US formally lost its 26-year elimination status.
